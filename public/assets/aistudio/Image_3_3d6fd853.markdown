## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-authority, premium corporate aesthetic tailored for professional U.S. visa consulting. It projects credibility, procedural precision, and international mobility, assuring high-net-worth applicants, students, and corporate travelers of rigorous compliance and expertise.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Style Archetype:** Modern Premium Corporate mixed with High-Trust Civic Clarity.
- **Visual Character:** Architectural structure, restrained density, micro-interactions calibrated for reassurance, and clear legibility.
- **Conversion Philosophy:** Frictionless forms, prominent compliance badges, unambiguous fee structures, and immediate trust indicators without hyperbolic claims.
- **Regulatory Integrity:** Dedicated visual slots for official legal disclosures, success criteria transparency, and strict Google Ads compliance.

## Colors

The palette balances diplomatic authority with contemporary digital clarity:

- **Primary (`#0B2545`):** Deep Diplomatic Navy. Serves as the anchor for headers, primary CTAs, footers, and foundational identity elements.
- **Secondary (`#1D63FF`):** Vibrant International Blue. Applied to active navigation, micro-actions, focus rings, and high-conversion interactive elements.
- **Tertiary / Accent (`#D4AF37`):** Warm Champagne Gold. Reserved strictly for premium service designations, official seal outlines, and milestone markers.
- **Neutral Core (`#0F172A` & `#334155`):** Deep slate tones delivering sharp contrast ratios exceeding WCAG AAA standards for editorial and form text.
- **Surfaces:** Pure crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) for core content cards, backed by layered slate surfaces (`#F8FAFC` to `#F1F5F9`) to define visual cadence.
- **Badges & Disclaimers:** Soft cobalt tints (`#EFF6FF`) and warm amber notices (`#FEF3C7`) for institutional advisories and legal declarations.

## Typography

The pairing combines **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines with **Inter** for dense UI strings and long-form advisory content. Both typefaces feature complete, native Vietnamese diacritics with balanced vertical metric bounds.

- **Headline Hierarchy:** Expressed in deep navy (`#0B2545`) with tight letter-spacing to emphasize authority and structural weight.
- **Reading Rhythm:** Body copy maintains a 1.5 to 1.55 line-height ratio in `#334155` to ensure relaxed scanability across complex immigration policies and requirements.
- **Labels & Disclaimers:** Rendered in medium weights with subtle tracking expansion for immediate comprehension in data tables and statutory footers.

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column fluid grid system anchors desktop experiences, collapsing to a single-column layout on mobile devices.

- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12 columns, 24px gutters, max content container width of 1240px centered within standard 48px canvas margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, 20px gutters, 32px canvas margins. Side-by-side assessment flows reflow into stacked cards.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px gutters, 20px side margins. High-priority forms switch to sticky bottom actions to maintain thumb-zone accessibility.
- **Vertical Cadence:** Strict 8px increment baseline grid for component interiors, scaling up to 40px/80px sectional separations to maintain executive whitespace.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses tonal surface transitions, low-contrast structural borders, and delicate ambient shadows rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Surface Tiers:** Baseline canvas sits at `#F8FAFC`. Active cards and interactive containers reside at `#FFFFFF`, framed by a precise border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`).
- **Ambient Elevation:** Primary floating components (e.g., sticky assessment widgets, modal sheets) use a multi-stage diffused shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.04), 0 12px 24px -4px rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.08)`.
- **Gold Accent Elevation:** Premium tier offerings utilize a soft, directional champagne glow: `0 0 0 1px #D4AF37, 0 8px 20px -4px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`.
- **Depth Transitions:** Hover states transition elevation via subtle Y-axis translation (-2px) and shadow spread refinement over 200ms cubic-bezier easing.

## Shapes

The interface balances corporate formality with modern software ergonomics:

- **Standard Containers:** Form inputs, action buttons, cards, and modal dialogs adopt an 8px (`0.5rem`) corner radius.
- **Large Surfaces:** Sectional spotlight blocks and hero cards utilize a 16px (`1rem`) corner radius.
- **Indicators & Badges:** Statutory status badges, visa category pills, and count chips utilize a fully rounded pill format (`9999px`) to distinguish meta-information from clickable blocks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Background `#0B2545`, text `#FFFFFF`, height 48px, horizontal padding 24px, 8px corner radius. On hover, background shifts to `#133E87` with subtle lift.
- **Conversion CTA (Direct Apply):** Background `#1D63FF`, text `#FFFFFF`, weight 600. Includes an explicit forward chevron icon.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background transparent, border `1.5px solid #0B2545`, text `#0B2545`.

### Input Fields & Selects
- **Base Geometry:** Height 48px, 8px corner radius, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`.
- **Focus State:** Border color `#1D63FF` with a coordinated `0 0 0 3px rgba(29, 99, 255, 0.15)` focus ring.
- **Labels & Validation:** Floating or top-aligned labels in `#334155` (`label-md`). Inline validation messages use distinct icons and specific advisory text rather than harsh full-width red blocks.

### Cards & Service Tiers
- **Structure:** Solid `#FFFFFF` interior, 24px internal padding, `1px solid #E2E8F0` border.
- **Highlighted Tier (US B1/B2, F1, EB-5):** Border upgraded to `1.5px solid #1D63FF`, augmented by an absolute-positioned badge at the top right in `#EFF6FF` with `#1D63FF` typography.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** Height 24px, padding 4px 10px, font size 12px, weight 600, pill-shaped.
- **Compliance Badge:** `#F1F5F9` background, `#334155` text, displaying official registration and verification credentials.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Control Sizing:** 20px x 20px box, 4px corner radius for checkboxes, circle for radios. Active state fills with `#1D63FF` displaying a crisp white indicator.

### Legal Disclaimers & Google Ads Transparency Block
- **Layout:** Dedicated container on `#F8FAFC`, delimited by `1px dashed #CBD5E1`.
- **Typography:** 13px text in `#475569` detailing non-affiliation with the U.S. government, processing timelines, and transparent service-fee policies.
